# PerFin — Privacy Notice
---
See also: [Terms of Use](terms.md)

PerFin is a personal, non-commercial application operated by
Matyáš Sedláček (the developer). It is used solely by its operator
as a private financial dashboard.

## What data is accessed

With explicit consent given through the bank's own authentication
flow, PerFin requests read-only access to account details, balances
and transaction history via Enable Banking (a licensed Account
Information Service Provider). No payment initiation is performed
and no credentials are ever seen or stored by this application.

## How data is stored and used

All retrieved data is stored exclusively on the operator's local
machine. No data is uploaded to any cloud service, sold, shared
with third parties, or used for marketing or profiling of any kind.

## Retention and withdrawal

Bank consents are limited in duration (as defined by each bank) and
can be withdrawn at any time via the bank's settings or by
requesting session deletion. Locally stored data can be erased by
deleting the local application files.
